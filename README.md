# Floorplanner - Design Your Dream Space

Drop in a floor plan, add your furniture (or paste a product link), and see what
actually fits — to scale, with clearances and walkways checked.

## Story

I'm 22 and I've moved a lot. Every time, I want my place set up so coming home
means I can actually focus (if i'm coding :P) or actually relax. floorplanner is 
dedicated to anyone who wants to make their space their own, without guessing whether
the sofa fits.
